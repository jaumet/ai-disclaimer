TRANSPARENCY WITH CONTEXT

Beyond detection:
declare how AI was used.

The EU AI Act establishes transparency obligations for certain AI systems and AI-generated or manipulated content, including machine-readable markings and disclosure requirements under Article 50.

AI USE DECLARED goes one step further. Rather than relying on detectors to guess whether AI was involved, it enables creators and organisations to state it openly: which tools were used, for which tasks, and to what extent.

Detectors may indicate possible AI involvement, but they cannot reconstruct the creative process or explain the respective contributions of humans and AI. A clear, public, human-readable and machine-readable self-declaration provides that missing context.

AI USE DECLARED is aligned with the transparency goals of the EU AI Act and is designed to complement—not replace—legally required markings and provenance systems.

A PROPOSED AI-USE CLASSIFICATION

A clear classification.
Not fine print.

Start with exactly one primary category, then add every qualifier that meaningfully describes the process. The result is readable at a glance and detailed when needed.

Choose one overall category

AI-MADE

Generative AI produced the main or essential part of the result.

Example: an AI-generated illustration art-directed by a person.

AI-ASSISTED

The work is human-led, with substantial AI contributions to ideas, writing, code, design or production.

Example: a human-designed product containing meaningful AI-generated code.

AI-EDITED

The original is human-made and AI appears later for correction, retouching, cleanup or post-production.

Example: a human photograph cleaned up with generative removal tools.

NO GENERATIVE AI

No generative AI created or transformed content; analytical AI may still be present.

Example: a human-designed app using a recommendation algorithm.

NO AI USED

No generative or analytical AI system was used to produce the project.

Example: a hand-coded website made without AI-powered tools or features.

Add the details that apply

HUMAN-REVIEWED

A person meaningfully reviewed the result before publication.

Example: an editor checked and corrected an AI-produced draft.

AI-TRANSLATED

A significant part of the text was translated using AI.

Example: website copy translated with an AI translation service.

SYNTHETIC VOICE

The project contains AI-generated narration, synthetic speech or a cloned voice.

Example: an explainer narrated using a licensed synthetic voice.

AI-GENERATED IMAGES

The project contains images created wholly or partly with generative AI.

Example: campaign artwork generated from prompts and then edited.

AI-GENERATED CODE

AI generated a meaningful portion of the code, beyond trivial autocomplete.

Example: AI drafted components that a developer reviewed and integrated.

AI-GENERATED VIDEO

The project contains video created or materially transformed with generative AI.

Example: an AI-generated reel, story, animation or video sequence.

AI-GENERATED TEXT

AI meaningfully helped write, synthesise, restructure or expand the text.

Example: AI expanded an outline into copy that a person revised.
Keep it coherent.NO AI USED may only be paired with HUMAN-REVIEWED.NO GENERATIVE AI cannot be paired with generative-AI details.When in doubt, choose the category that makes AI involvement more visible.
