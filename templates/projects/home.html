TRANSPARENCY WITH CONTEXT

Beyond marking:
explain how AI was used.

The EU AI Act requires machine-readable marking for certain AI-generated or manipulated outputs, together with human-readable disclosure in specific cases. These measures help machines and people recognise AI-generated content.

AI USE DECLARED has a different, complementary purpose: a voluntary, human-readable account of the role AI played across a project. It adds creative and organisational context that a technical mark or detector cannot provide.

It complements—not replaces—legal markings, provenance systems or any applicable obligation under Article 50 and the European Commission’s transparency guidelines.

The Transparency Pledge

A commitment to truthful, clear and responsible disclosure of how AI was used.

AI USE DECLARED records a personal or organisational self-declaration. It does not imply independent verification or certification.
