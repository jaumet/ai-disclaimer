TRANSPARENCY WITH CONTEXT

Beyond detection:
declare how AI was used.

Detectors may estimate whether AI was involved, but they cannot explain the creative process. AI USE DECLARED provides that missing context through a public, human-readable and machine-readable declaration.

As a voluntary system, AI USE DECLARED complements—not replaces—the transparency obligations, machine-readable markings and provenance systems required by the EU AI Act and its Article 50 transparency guidelines.
