TRANSPARENCY WITH CONTEXT

Beyond detection:
declare how AI was used.

The EU AI Act establishes transparency obligations for certain AI systems and AI-generated or manipulated content, including machine-readable markings and disclosure requirements under Article 50.

AI USE DECLARED goes one step further. Rather than relying on detectors to guess whether AI was involved, it enables creators and organisations to state it openly: which tools were used, for which tasks, and to what extent.

Detectors may indicate possible AI involvement, but they cannot reconstruct the creative process or explain the respective contributions of humans and AI. A clear, public, human-readable and machine-readable self-declaration provides that missing context.

AI USE DECLARED is aligned with the transparency goals of the EU AI Act and is designed to complement—not replace—legally required markings and provenance systems.
