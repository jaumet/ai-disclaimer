The Transparency Pledge.

Publishing how something was made is an act of respect—for audiences, collaborators and the work itself. Everyone joining AI USE DECLARED commits to these principles.

▶
Listen to the pledge
  1. 01

    Tell the truth.

    I will describe the role of AI accurately and will not minimise, exaggerate or conceal its contribution.

  2. 02

    Choose the clearest badge.

    When more than one category seems possible, I will choose the one that makes AI’s involvement most visible.

  3. 03

    Give credit responsibly.

    I will not use transparency badges to erase the work, rights or authorship of collaborators and source creators.

  4. 04

    Keep the record current.

    If the project or its process changes materially, I will update its disclosure.

  5. 05

    Do not imply independent verification.

    I understand that AI USE DECLARED records a personal or organisational declaration. It does not independently verify accuracy, legality, safety, quality or ownership.

  6. 06

    Correct mistakes openly.

    If I discover that a disclosure is misleading or wrong, I will correct it promptly rather than leave a false impression.

✦

Transparency is part of the craft.

By creating or accessing an AI USE DECLARED account, you affirm this pledge and take responsibility for the disclosures you publish.
